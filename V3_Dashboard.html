Source: Screened National mental-health strategies, legislation and WHO-AIMS/WHO country reports (primary, English-language screen), aggregated from 630 individual documents across 126 countries. Documents are classified by MNSS categories: mental disorder, neurological/neurodevelopmental, substance use, or self-harm, and can be filtered above. "Acknowledges differences" means at least one screened document notes a sex/gender/orientation-based difference in mental health policy; "Policy recommendations" shows any policy document that addresses SGO differences and contains a concrete policy, plan or legal provision keeping these differences in mind. Hover a country for the underlying document counts. Countries not present in the underlying dataset are shown unshaded. Tuvalu is included in the dataset but is too small to render at world-map scale, so it does not appear on the map above.
