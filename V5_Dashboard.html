Sex and Gender Disparities in Mental Health – What Are Our Policies Doing?

Mental health conditions, neurological conditions, substance use and self-harm (together, MNSS) affect people differently by sex, gender and sexual orientation (together, SGO). Depression, for example, is more common in women than in men, yet research and policy rarely look beyond biological sex. Recognising these differences is not enough on its own; policies also need concrete actions to address them. We reviewed national mental health policies for both: do they acknowledge SGO differences, and do they act on them?
